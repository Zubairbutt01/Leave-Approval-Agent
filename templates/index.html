What the assistant can do for you

Everything about your leave in one place. Ask in plain words, and the assistant guides you through it.

Request leave

Share your start date, end date, and a short reason. The assistant checks your balance first, then sends the request to your manager.

Check your leave balance

See how many of your 14 annual leave days are left. Pending and approved requests are already taken off.

Track a request

Ask for the status of a request with its number. You will see if it is still pending, approved, or rejected.

How it works

Four steps from your first message to the decision in your inbox.

  1. Start a conversation

    Open the assistant and enter your Employee ID.

  2. Share your dates and reason

    Give the first day, the last day, and a short reason for your leave.

  3. Your manager decides

    The request goes to your manager by email. They approve or reject it with one click.

  4. You get an email

    The decision arrives in your inbox with your dates and remaining balance.

You will be notified when your manager decides

You do not need to come back and ask. As soon as your manager approves or rejects your request, the assistant sends you an email.

  • You get an email whether the request is approved or rejected.
  • The email goes to the address saved in your employee record.
  • You can still ask the assistant for the status at any time.

Good to know before you start

A few simple rules, so your request goes through the first time.

Employee ID
Use the ID assigned to you, for example E001.
Annual leave
Every employee has 14 days of annual leave per year.
Start date
Leave can start today or later. Dates in the past are not accepted.
Balance
You cannot request more days than you have left.
Who decides
Only your manager approves or rejects leave. The assistant never decides.

Frequently asked questions

Who approves my leave?

Your manager. The assistant collects your request and sends it on, but it never approves or rejects leave itself.

How will I know the decision?

You will get an email as soon as your manager decides. You can also ask the assistant for the status at any time, using your request number.

I did not get the email. What should I do?

Check your spam or junk folder first. Then ask the assistant for the status of your request number. If the email address in your employee record is wrong, ask your administrator to correct it.

Can I request more days than I have left?

No. The assistant checks your balance before it creates a request, and tells you how many days you have left.

What can the assistant not do?

It only helps with leave requests. It cannot answer other questions, and it cannot change a decision your manager has made.
